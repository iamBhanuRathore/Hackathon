1. The "Ghost" Stockout
"Telling customers 'No' while the warehouse is full"
What happened: 542 orders unfulfilled, but distributor inventory shows 100% stock was sitting on hand.
Why it matters: ₹51.66 Lakhs in instant sales lost simply because sales reps cannot see distributor stock in real-time.
What to do next: Auto-route orders directly to distributor dispatch queues instead of manual confirmations.
How to measure: Zero unfulfilled orders when Available Qty > 0; 100% Ghost Stockout Elimination in 30 days.
🏪
2. The "Silent" Churn
"1,381 stores stopped buying, but no alarm bells rang"
What happened: 46% of all retailers are still marked "Active" in CRM, but have placed 0 orders in >60 days.
Why it matters: ₹17.20 Crores annual volume at risk. Stores don't complain—they silently switch to competitors.
What to do next: Trigger automated WhatsApp re-engagement directives with high-lift LED/Geyser schemes at Day 21 of inactivity.
How to measure: Reactivate 200 Tier A/B accounts within 14 days; recover +₹1.43 Cr/month run rate.
🏃
3. The "Drive-By" Reps
"Reps logging fake visits to hit activity quotas"
What happened: 3 reps (SP005, SP017, SP023) logged 4,623 visits, but 962 lasted <10 mins and 1,414 lacked GPS proof. Strike rate was only 16.5% (vs 59% avg).
Why it matters: Sales reps are our most expensive growth asset. Wasted visits cost ₹1.85 Cr and leave good retailers unserviced.
What to do next: Prune non-productive stops into bi-weekly tele-sales; enforce mandatory 15-minute geofenced check-ins.
How to measure: Lift field strike rate from 16.5% to 45%+; cut drive-by visits by 80%.
💰
4. Dead Stock vs Starving Stores
"Working capital locked up in slow-moving dust"
What happened: 16,086 units (₹20.02 Cr value) are aged 90 to 180+ days in distributor depots while high-demand stores face order cuts.
Why it matters: Depreciating stock ties up distributor cash credit, preventing them from buying fast-moving SKUs.
What to do next: Reallocate aged inventory across territories using targeted trade rebate schemes (e.g., SCH02) before obsolescence.
How to measure: Liquidate 35% of 90d+ aged stock within 45 days, freeing ₹7 Cr in working capital.
